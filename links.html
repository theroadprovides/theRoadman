THE ROAD PROVIDES

The Road Provides is an open-ended experiment built around one character, many roads, and the people who decide to enter them. The Roadman is the first chapter.

01 / THE PROJECT
One character.
Many roads.

The Roadman is a character moving through an unknown road. There is no single destination and no predetermined ending.

The project is built as an experience rather than simply an image. Each person who enters becomes part of the road and discovers what exists beyond the next step.

02 / THE COIN
$ROAD
THE ROAD PROVIDES — SOLANA

$ROAD is the coin created for The Road Provides. It exists as part of the project's on-chain layer and as one of the entry points into the first chapter.

The project does not promise a price, return, or financial outcome. What happens on the market belongs to the market. The Road is the experience.
